# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 10, 2025

At Journeo, we take your privacy seriously. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

## Information We Collect

### Location Information
Journeo uses your location data to provide you with the best travel experience:
- GPS location for real-time navigation
- To show nearby points of interest
- To generate AI-powered route suggestions

Location data is only collected while using the app and is not shared without your permission.

### Account Information
- Email address
- Name and surname
- Profile photo (optional)

### Usage Data
- App usage statistics
- Your preferences and settings
- Travel history

## How We Use Data

The data we collect is used for:
- Providing app functionality
- Offering personalized recommendations
- Improving user experience
- Providing customer support

## Data Security

- All data is stored encrypted on the Firebase platform
- Secure transmission with SSL/TLS protocols
- Regular security audits
- Industry-standard security measures

## Third-Party Services

Journeo uses the following third-party services:
- **Firebase (Google)**: Authentication and data storage
- **Google Maps**: Map and location services
- **OpenAI**: AI-powered recommendations

These services are subject to their own privacy policies.

## Data Retention

- Your data is retained while your account is active
- When you request account deletion, your data is permanently deleted within 30 days
- Data required for legal obligations may be retained

## Your Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the following rights:
- Right to access your data
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure (right to be forgotten)
- Right to object to processing
- Right to data portability

## Children's Privacy

Journeo is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from this age group.

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions:
- Email: privacy@journeo.ai
- Web: https://journeo.ai/contact
