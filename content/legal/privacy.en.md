## 3. Personal data we collect

We collect only what we need to operate Journeo. Depending on how you use the Service, this may include:

### 3.1 Account and profile data

- Email address and authentication credentials (password is stored in hashed form by our auth provider; we do not see your plain-text password)
- Username (unique, used to find and add friends)
- Display / full name
- Profile photo (avatar)
- Timezone
- Preferred language / locale
- Account identifiers generated by our systems

### 3.2 Travel and content you create

- Trip titles, destinations (city/country), dates, cover images, and trip status
- Travel preferences (style, pace, diet, budget tier, interests, accessibility settings)
- Itinerary days and stops (places, notes, order, booking-related links you add)
- Packing lists, flights you add, and trip expenses
- Friendships / trip memberships and roles (owner, editor, viewer)
- Planner chat messages and prompts you send to generate or refine itineraries
- Affiliate click events when you open partner booking links we generate

### 3.3 Location data

With your permission, we may collect approximate and/or precise device location **while you use the app** to:

- show your position on the map
- tailor trip planning to where you are
- support features such as city detection for voice planning
- **live location sharing with trip companions** — only when **you** start a time-limited live-share session for a trip (for example 1 / 4 / 8 hours or until end of day). While that session is active and the app is in the foreground, your approximate/precise coordinates may be visible to other members of **that trip**. Sharing stops when you turn it off, when the session expires, or when the app goes to the background. We do **not** request “always” / background location permission for this feature, and we do not share your live location with non-members or for advertising.

We design location use around **when-in-use** access and balanced accuracy. We do **not** continuously track your location in the background for advertising. Continuous high-accuracy tracking is not used except for narrowly scoped navigation-style use cases when enabled, and is cancelled when that mode ends.

You can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings. Some map and planning features may work with reduced accuracy or without location.

### 3.4 Microphone and speech

With your permission, we may access the microphone and on-device / system speech recognition so you can plan trips by voice. Audio is used to convert speech into text for planning; we do not sell voice recordings for advertising.

### 3.5 Camera and photo library

With your permission, we may access the camera or photo library so you can set a profile picture. Images you choose may be uploaded to our storage and associated with your account.

### 3.6 Notifications

If you enable notifications, we store device push tokens (for example FCM tokens) and notification preference settings so we can deliver trip-related and product notifications. You can change notification permissions in device settings and, where available, in-app preferences.

### 3.7 Device, diagnostics, and usage data

- App version, device type / OS, language, and similar technical signals needed to run the Service
- Crash and performance diagnostics (for example via Sentry), which may include device metadata and stack traces
- Analytics events about feature usage (for example onboarding completed, trip created, AI plan generated) via Firebase Analytics — **without** sending content we treat as unnecessary personal identifiers in event payloads
- IP address and request metadata processed by our servers and hosting providers for security, rate limiting, and abuse prevention
- Local offline copies of trip data stored on your device (SQLite / Drift) so the app can work offline

### 3.8 Payments and entitlements (when enabled)

If you purchase a subscription or in-app unlock, payment is processed by Apple, Google, and/or RevenueCat. We receive entitlement / subscription status needed to unlock features; we do **not** receive your full payment card number.

### 3.9 Information from third-party sign-in (when you choose it)

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive limited account information those providers share with us (typically an identifier and email / name, depending on your provider settings) to create or link your Journeo account.

### 3.10 Information we do **not** sell

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do not rent your contact list or trip content to data brokers.

## 4. How we use personal data

We use personal data to:

1. **Provide the Service** — create accounts, authenticate you, store and sync trips, show maps and itineraries, enable collaboration with trip members and friends  
2. **Generate AI itineraries and related content** — send relevant trip and preference context to our backend, which calls AI model providers to produce structured travel plans; verify places against place databases/geocoders; generate certain city cover / icon images under controlled, cached pipelines  
3. **Personalize** — apply your preferences, locale, timezone, and trip settings  
4. **Communicate** — send transactional messages and (with permission) push notifications  
5. **Improve reliability and safety** — diagnostics, debugging, fraud/abuse prevention, rate limits  
6. **Measure product usage** — aggregated or event-level analytics to understand which features work  
7. **Affiliate attribution** — record that a booking link was opened so partners can attribute referrals; this supports our business model  
8. **Comply with law** — respond to lawful requests; enforce Terms; protect rights and safety  
9. **Honor your requests** — data export, account deletion, preference updates

**Legal bases (where GDPR/KVKK apply):** contract performance; consent (for example optional permissions such as location, microphone, camera, notifications, and certain analytics/marketing where required); legitimate interests (security, product improvement, fraud prevention — balanced against your rights); and legal obligation.

## 5. AI processing

Journeo uses artificial intelligence to help generate and refine travel itineraries and, in limited cases, city imagery used as trip covers or icons.

- AI calls are made from **our servers**, not directly from your device with exposed model API keys.
- Inputs may include destination, dates, preferences, and planner conversation context you provide.
- Outputs may include suggested places and schedules. Some places may be marked unverified if we cannot confirm them against place data.
- AI providers process prompts/responses as needed to return results. We configure providers under our agreements and do not use AI outputs to build advertising profiles about you.
- City cover / icon generation follows a generate-once, cache, and reuse model for a city key so the same city image can be shared across users. This is not a portrait of you and is not tied to selling your identity.

AI suggestions can be wrong. Always verify critical travel information independently.

## 6. How we share data

We share personal data only as described below:

### 6.1 Service providers (processors)

| Category | Examples of providers | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Backend / database / auth | Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage) | Account, trip data, sync, avatars, certain images |
| Hosting | Google Cloud Run (and related Google Cloud infrastructure) | API hosting |
| Maps | Mapbox | Maps, styles, geocoding fallback, offline map tooling |
| Places | Google Places API (server-side) | Place verification / resolution (cache-first) |
| AI models | Google Gemini | Itinerary generation |
| Image generation (limited) | fal.ai | City cover images (server-side, cached) |
| Free / public image sources | Wikipedia / Wikimedia; Pexels | POI or destination imagery |
| Weather | Open-Meteo | Weather forecasts for trip dates (API-keyless weather data) |
| Analytics | Firebase Analytics | Product analytics |
| Crash / observability | Sentry | Crash and error monitoring |
| Push notifications | Firebase Cloud Messaging | Push delivery |
| Payments (when enabled) | Apple / Google / RevenueCat | In-app purchases and subscription status |
| Affiliate partners | Booking, Kiwi, Skyscanner, Viator (and similar) | When you click a booking / travel partner link we generate |

Providers only receive what is needed for their function.

### 6.2 Other users you choose to interact with

If you add friends, accept requests, or share a trip, other members may see profile details you share (such as username / name / avatar) and trip content according to membership roles.

If you enable **live location sharing** on a trip (Section 3.3), trip companions can see your location for the duration of that session only.

### 6.2.1 Safety reports and blocks

You can **block** another user and **report** abusive or inappropriate behaviour from within the app. Blocking stops further friend requests and related social contact from that user on your side. Reports are reviewed by Journeo and may include the reported user’s account id, your account id, the reason you selected, and any optional details you provide — used only for safety / abuse handling.

### 6.3 Legal and safety

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect Journeo, our users, or the public from harm, fraud, or security threats.

### 6.4 Business transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate protections.

### 6.5 No sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information as that term is commonly understood under US state privacy laws. We also do not share personal information for cross-app / cross-site advertising tracking (“tracking” as defined by Apple) as a business purpose. If that ever changes, we will update this Policy and obtain any required consent (including App Tracking Transparency where applicable).

## 9. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, including:

- **Account data** — while your account remains active  
- **Trip and collaboration data** — while needed to provide the Service to you and other trip members  
- **Caches** (for example AI plan cache, places cache, weather cache, city images) — according to product TTLs / operational needs; some caches are global and not tied to a single user identity  
- **Affiliate / security logs** — for a limited period needed for attribution, abuse prevention, and compliance  
- **Diagnostics / analytics** — according to the retention settings of those tools

When you delete your account (Section 11), we delete or anonymize personal data we control that is no longer needed, subject to limited exceptions (legal holds, shared-trip continuity described below, backups that age out, and data that must be retained by law).

**Shared trips:** If you own a trip that has other members and you delete your account, ownership may be transferred to another member so their trip is not destroyed. Content needed for that shared trip may therefore remain available to remaining members even after your account is deleted. Trips with no other members are removed with your account.

## 10. Security

We use industry-standard measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including:

- encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- access controls and authentication

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use a strong unique password and keep your device updated.
